# Feedback & Bug Log

Quick-capture scratchpad for bugs and feedback as you hit them (real-device
testing, UX testers, your own play-testing). Jot a one-liner, don't worry
about formatting or detail — flesh it out later if it turns into real work.

Periodically (ask Claude, or it'll notice), entries here get triaged into
`PROJECT_STATUS.md`'s "Known gaps"/"Backlog" sections and cleared out. This
file should stay short-lived — if it's getting long, that's the cue to
triage.
